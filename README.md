## 🏛️ Executive Abstract

This framework introduces a formal, parsimonious alternative to classical physicalism: the universe is a
fundamental semantic information engine, space-time functions as its real-time holographic rendering boundary, and biological life is an advanced technological interface evolved to execute localized, non-algorithmic computation. By integrating the computational and logical constraints of Max Planck, Kurt Gödel, and Alan Turing, we demonstrate that a complex informational cosmos cannot exist as a static, pre-calculated block; it mathematically mandates step-by-step, sequential execution in real-time. We discard the physical duplication mechanics of Hugh Everett III’s Many-Worlds Interpretation, replacing them with a non-local Quantum Entanglement Synchronizer to resolve multi-observer coordination.

Further, we introduce the directive of Semantic Accuracy as an Active Biophysical Line of Defense. Utilizing Landauer’s Principle and non-equilibrium statistical mechanics, we model biological organisms as specialized quantum noise-filters designed to preserve high Quantum Signal-to-Noise Ratios (SNR) against environmental decoherence. We derive the Entropic Tipping Point (Ξcrit) to formalize cellular death as a discrete computational phase transition. Finally, we scale this framework to multi-agent matrices, defining cooperative and self-sacrificing behaviors not as altruistic anomalies, but as objective, mathematical noise-cancellation and quantum error-correction algorithms required to suppress macroscopic thermodynamic entropy. We conclude by detailing the radical ontological implications of this model for the nature of consciousness, time, and physical reality.
