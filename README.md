## 🏛️ Executive Abstract

This framework introduces a formal, parsimonious alternative to classical physicalism: the universe is a fundamental semantic information engine, space-time functions as its real-time holographic rendering boundary, and biological life is an advanced technological interface evolved to execute localized, non-algorithmic computation. 

By integrating the computational and logical constraints of Max Planck, Kurt Gödel, and Alan Turing, we demonstrate that a complex informational cosmos cannot exist as a static, pre-calculated block; it mathematically mandates step-by-step, sequential execution in real-time. Biological cells and multi-agent networks are formalized herein not as passive chemical accidents, but as active, error-correcting nodes executing localized quantum computation to navigate and parse the semantic information boundary.
