## Abstract

This framework introduces a formal alternative to classical physicalism: the universe is a fundamental semantic information engine, space-time functions as its real-time holographic rendering boundary, and biological life is an interface evolved to execute non-algorithmic computation. 

Integrating constraints from Planck, Gödel, Turing, Faggin, and D'Ariano, it replaces Many-Worlds duplication with a non-local Quantum Entanglement Synchronizer. It models organisms as quantum noise-filters preserving high Signal-to-Noise Ratios (SNR) via Landauer's Principle, defines the Entropic Tipping Point (Ξcrit) for cellular death, and scales cooperation to multi-agent matrices as quantum error-correction algorithms that suppress entropy.
