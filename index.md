## Abstract

This framework introduces a formal alternative to classical physicalism, modeling the universe as a fundamental semantic information engine where space-time acts as its holographic rendering boundary and biological life serves as an interface for non-algorithmic computation. By integrating constraints from Planck, Gödel, Turing, Faggin, and D'Ariano, it replaces Many-Worlds duplication mechanics with a non-local Quantum Entanglement Synchronizer. 

It further models biological organisms as quantum noise-filters preserving high Signal-to-Noise Ratios (SNR) via Landauer's Principle, defines the Entropic Tipping Point ($\Xi_{\text{crit}}$) for cellular death, and scales these principles to multi-agent matrices as quantum error-correction algorithms suppressing thermodynamic entropy.
